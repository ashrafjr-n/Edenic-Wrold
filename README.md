# Edenic World

An educational web app for kids aged 5–9. Three mascots — Pinki, Nova and Bloo — teach short video-led courses (real clips, compressed to 720p H.264 by `tools/reels/encode.sh`), and the site's four tabs are Home, Learn, Watch and Play (a phone gets them as a bottom bar of filled icons, a wider screen in the header). Play is Bloo's sky — one big clay stage with clouds, Bloo standing on the top edge of three game cards and the bank of stars in the corner — clay tiles in each game's colour with its picture in a white-rimmed window: jigsaw puzzles (each picture is a frame of one of the series' songs, and a finished puzzle offers that song in a pop-up player — fourteen songs, 720p H.264 in `public/assets/watch/songs/`), Memory Match, and Find — a photo hunt in a pre-rendered 3D place: Nova's list at the top shows the two things to find (three in a harder stage — tap one to hear its word), and the child drags to look round Nova's kitchen, whose room, counter and blurred near things slide at their own speeds (a parallax, in a wide frame and a taller phone frame), so in stage 1 the carrot peeks out from behind a fruit bowl and the broccoli from behind a flower jug, in stage 2 (Nova's candy-pink garden) a flower behind a bush and a leaf behind a watering can, and in stage 3 (Bloo's sunny farm) the cat shows only its ears and tail over a hay stack and the dog's head over some crates — Bloo's animals are the videos' own pictures — and in stage 4 (Pinki's felt shape land) a park of felt circles, squares, triangles, rectangles and ovals hides the heart's two round tops behind near felt flowers and the diamond's point behind a felt cube, between two triangles' points; hints are seen first (they stir, twinkle, the arrow to the nearest pulses, at last they glow) and the rustle, from its side of the screen, is only an extra; each find flies into its slot on the list and gets a green tick, and with all found the camera pulls focus, a flash, and a photo of them together goes into a nine-page photo album, which is also the game's stage picker (stages 1–4 built, five to come; each stage's friend asks and cheers). A game opens for two of the stars a child earns in Learn: a locked card's picture is frosted under a white clay padlock with two empty star sockets; Play keeps a bank of the stars left, and when the child chooses a game, two stars fly from the bank into the sockets, the padlock springs open and the game stays open for good; without stars enough, Nova explains how to earn one and offers the way to Learn, and a locked game's pages send the child back to Play; Watch leads with the series' YouTube episodes, which take turns in one hero (a pink clay panel with Pinki beside the episode's picture), then the series' songs in puzzle order (white clay cards, each with its name and the very frame its puzzle was cut from), and then the lessons' short videos. Every course's lessons happen in a pre-rendered 3D world of their own (Pinki's felt-land shape garden and pastel colour land, Nova's kitchen and her candy-pink garden, Bloo's sunny farm for Animals and his floating island of the year for Seasons and Weather) — one island, laid out the same in all four seasons, each lesson turning it into its season; after the weather's reel the camera walks on while the weather rolls in over that same island (clouds slide in and a light rain starts, the sun comes out, the wind picks up, snow begins to fall), and the weather keeps moving behind its steps as seamless looping clips. That course's page walks through the year, each stop standing in its own season with its weather's things beside it. Every course page is itself a 3D world of stops where scrolling moves the camera: each activity is a camera shot of it, the moves between them are rendered camera moves, and the lesson ends there with the friend celebrating. Finishing a course opens its gold chest, which gives the course's star; each friend's pages carry a star pod under the back button (a white clay chip, "1/2", that opens into one slot per course — a pale clay star or the gold one), which opens by itself when a new star arrives: the star comes up out of the chest and flies into its empty slot, and the chest stays open, empty. A course whose star is already given opens its chest without the opening again — also when its last lesson is played once more. Opening a lesson shows one whole-screen loading cover (the friend's classroom wall and three hopping clay magnets) for at least a second and a half while the reel and every picture of the lesson's world load behind it, and the done screen downloads the next lesson's reel, so Next plays it straight from the browser's cache. Arabic and Kurdish fonts are fetched only on a page in that language. The world's frames are cached by the browser for good (their URLs carry a content hash), so a second visit loads them instantly. The first visit of a session opens on a short clay splash (the logo's two words bounce in and its star spins on, plain CSS, about a second and a half), and photos blur in from a tiny preview while they load. Learn has sound: every taught word is spoken (American English, made with the Kokoro text-to-speech model), and every button, letter, find, feeding, finished lesson, opened chest, cloud transition and photo snap has its own soft toy-like sound, synthesised from scratch — both are generated by `tools/sounds/` and played with the Web Audio API. The interface reads in English, Arabic and Badini Kurdish.

## Tech Stack

Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS v4 · Zustand · Lucide icons · Web Audio

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).

## Sounds

The clips live in `public/assets/audio/` and are made, not downloaded:

```bash
python3 tools/sounds/sfx.py [id …]               # sound effects → audio/sfx/ (needs numpy, ffmpeg)
venv/bin/python tools/sounds/words.py <models>   # spoken words → audio/words/ (kokoro-onnx; see the script)
```

After remaking a clip, bump `V` in `src/lib/cue.ts` so browsers fetch it again.
