# Edenic World

An educational web app for kids aged 5–9. Three mascots — Pinki, Nova and Bloo — teach short video-led courses (real clips, compressed to 720p H.264 by `tools/reels/encode.sh`), and Discover opens on two clay doors: Watch (a white clay TV playing the newest episode, Pinki beside it) and Play (the three games fanned like a hand of cards, Bloo beside them). Play shows three game cards — clay tiles in each game's colour with its picture in a white-rimmed window: jigsaw puzzles (each picture is a frame of one of the series' songs, and a finished puzzle offers that song in a pop-up player — fourteen songs, 720p H.264 in `public/assets/watch/songs/`), Memory Match, and Find, a 3D game still to come (its card shows Nova's kitchen counter). A game opens for two of the stars a child earns in Learn: a locked card's picture is frosted under a white clay padlock with two empty star sockets; Play keeps a bank of the stars left, and when the child chooses a game, two stars fly from the bank into the sockets, the padlock springs open and the game stays open for good; without stars enough, Nova explains how to earn one and offers the way to Learn, and a locked game's pages send the child back to Play; Watch leads with the series' YouTube episodes, which take turns in one hero, then the series' songs (song n is puzzle n's, its card the very frame the puzzle was cut from), and then the lessons' short videos. Every course's lessons happen in a pre-rendered 3D world of their own (Pinki's felt-land shape garden and pastel colour land, Nova's kitchen and her candy-pink garden, Bloo's sunny farm for Animals and his floating island of the year for Seasons and Weather) — each season's lessons turn the whole island into that season, and after the weather's reel it rains, shines, blows or snows there. Every course page is itself a 3D world of stops where scrolling moves the camera: each activity is a camera shot of it, the moves between them are rendered camera moves, and the lesson ends there with the friend celebrating. Finishing a course opens its gold chest, which gives the course's star; each friend's pages carry a star pod under the back button (a white clay chip, "1/2", that opens into one slot per course — a pale clay star or the gold one), which opens by itself when a new star arrives. Opening a lesson shows one whole-screen loading cover (the friend's classroom wall and three hopping clay magnets) for at least a second and a half while the reel and every picture of the lesson's world load behind it, and the done screen downloads the next lesson's reel, so Next plays it straight from the browser's cache. Arabic and Kurdish fonts are fetched only on a page in that language. The world's frames are cached by the browser for good (their URLs carry a content hash), so a second visit loads them instantly. The first visit of a session opens on a short clay splash (the logo's two words bounce in and its star spins on, plain CSS, about a second and a half), and photos blur in from a tiny preview while they load. Learn has sound: every taught word is spoken (American English, made with the Kokoro text-to-speech model), and every button, letter, find, feeding, finished lesson, opened chest and cloud transition has its own soft toy-like sound, synthesised from scratch — both are generated by `tools/sounds/` and played with the Web Audio API. The interface reads in English, Arabic and Badini Kurdish.

## Tech Stack

Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS v4 · Zustand · Lucide icons · Web Audio

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).

## Sounds

The clips live in `public/assets/audio/` and are made, not downloaded:

```bash
python3 tools/sounds/sfx.py [id …]               # sound effects → audio/sfx/ (needs numpy, ffmpeg)
venv/bin/python tools/sounds/words.py <models>   # spoken words → audio/words/ (kokoro-onnx; see the script)
```

After remaking a clip, bump `V` in `src/lib/cue.ts` so browsers fetch it again.
